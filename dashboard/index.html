What these figures include

Sales include usable merchandise rows even when CustomerID is missing. Exact duplicate source rows, invalid values, non-merchandise codes and inconsistent cancellation signs are excluded under the documented policy. Credits are negative recorded transactions; the data does not prove that each credit matches a particular sale. Net sales is not profit. Internal values use four decimal places; amounts on screen are rounded to two.

Recency and frequency groups

Fixed R/F rules; monetary value is reported, not used to assign a group

Frequency counts distinct positive-sale invoices. Monetary value is gross positive purchase spend, not net of credits. These are descriptive groups, not predictions.

Read the grouping rules

Rules run in this order: recency at most 30 days and frequency at least 5; recency over 90 days and frequency at least 2; other buyers with frequency at least 5; other repeat buyers; then one-time buyers. Recency is measured from the fixed cutoff, not today's date.

Limits that matter

  • The source ends on 9 December 2011. The final month is not comparable with a complete calendar month.
  • Missing customer IDs reduce customer-analysis coverage, but do not automatically remove a sale.
  • Credit notes are not a verified return rate. They may refer to sales outside the observed window.
  • There is no cost, margin, campaign exposure or inventory data here. This analysis cannot calculate profit or causal business impact.
  • The source has no reliable category hierarchy. Product comparisons use the original stock code and a representative description.
